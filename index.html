The Breakdown Skin for Video.js v10

A custom Video.js v10 player skin and active learning canvas with a synchronized 2.5D spatial field underneath.

Design Thinking: Most video players float controls directly over footage, dimming the picture on pause. But in technical walkthroughs, the paused frame is precisely what the viewer is studying. Breakdown moves controls to a dedicated grounded card, segmented by chapters, and connects playback to an interactive 2.5D spatial field underneath.

Unobscured Frame Controls sit on their own card below the video—never on the picture.
2.5D Spatial Field Synchronized isometric systems, route animations, and real-time state.
Step-Pause Mode Video stops at chapter checkpoints for hands-on comprehension.
Keyboard & A11y Full shortcut suite (K, M, F, J, L, <, >) and high-contrast visible focus.
